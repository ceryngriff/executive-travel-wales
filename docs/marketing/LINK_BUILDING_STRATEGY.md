# Link Building Strategy for Executive Travel Wales

## 🔗 Why Backlinks Matter for SEO

Backlinks (links from other websites to yours) are the #2 ranking factor after content quality. Think of them as "votes" for your site. More quality links = higher rankings.

**Important:** Quality > Quantity. One link from a reputable site is worth 100 from spam sites.

- **Domain Authority:** Low (new domain)
- **Target:** Get 20-50 quality backlinks in 6 months

## 🎯 Link Building Strategy (Phases)

### Phase 1: Local Business Directories (Week 1-2)
**Goal:** Get listed on major UK business directories

These are **high-authority, easy wins** that all local businesses should do.

**Free Directories to Submit To:**

1. **Google Business Profile** (MOST IMPORTANT)
   - Visit: https://google.com/business
   - Verify business in Cardiff
   - Add complete information
   - This gets you backlink + local ranking boost

2. **Bing Business Profile**
   - Visit: https://bingplaces.com
   - Similar to Google Business
   - Takes 5 minutes

3. **NHS Business Services**
   - Visit: https://www.businesssupport.wales/
   - Free listing for Welsh businesses
   - High authority = good for rankings

4. **UK Business Directories:**
   - Yell.com (free listing option)
   - ThomsonLocal.com
   - ClassifiedAds.com
   - BrightLocal Directory
   - Local.com

**Time Required:** 3-4 hours total
**Link Value:** High (authority domains)
**Expected Impact:** 5-10 quality backlinks

---

### Phase 2: Local Partnership Links (Week 2-4)
**Goal:** Get links from complementary local businesses

**Target businesses (Cardiff/South Wales):**
- Hotels and accommodation
- Event venues (wedding venues, conference centers)
- Airport shuttle services
- Tour companies
- Travel agencies
- Car rental companies
- Restaurants with event spaces

**How to approach:**
1. Find 10-15 local businesses in same industry/related industries
2. Write personalized email:
   ```
   Subject: Partnership Opportunity - Executive Travel Wales + [Their Business]

   Hi [Name],

   I noticed you serve clients in [Cardiff/South Wales region]. 
   I run Executive Travel Wales, providing premium transport for 
   [their customer's needs - e.g., hotel guests, event attendees].
   
   Would you be interested in a reciprocal link partnership? 
   I can link to your business from our partners page, and you 
   could link to us if our services complement yours.
   
   Let me know if you'd like to chat!
   
   Best regards,
   [Your Name]
   ```

3. Follow up after 1 week if no response

**Time Required:** 10-15 hours (research + outreach)
**Link Value:** Medium (local authority)
**Expected Impact:** 5-10 more backlinks

### Phase 3: Content-Based Links (Month 2-3)
**Goal:** Create content worth linking to

**Content Ideas:**
- "Top 10 Hidden Gems in South Wales" (tourist guide)
- "Best Hotels in Cardiff for Conferences" (with links to hotels)
- "Safe Travel Tips for Solo Travelers in Wales"
- "Group Travel Planning Guide"
- "Airport Transfer Safety Checklist"

**Strategy:**
1. Create detailed, original content
2. Promote content to relevant websites
3. Ask: "I created a guide that would help your audience. Can you link to it?"
4. Offer to link back to them

**Where to promote:**
- Travel blogs
- Tourism websites
- Business directories
- Industry forums
- Social media groups

**Time Required:** 20-30 hours (writing + outreach)
**Link Value:** High (contextual links)
**Expected Impact:** 10-15 quality backlinks

---

### Phase 4: Guest Post Links (Month 3-4)
**Goal:** Write articles for other websites and link back to yourself

**Where to pitch guest posts:**
- Welsh business blogs
- Travel and tourism blogs
- Local news websites
- Industry publications
- Medium.com (free blog platform)

**Article Ideas:**
- "Why Professional Transport Matters for Corporate Events"
- "The Hidden Costs of Cheap Travel Services"
- "How to Plan Safe Group Transport"
- "executive Travel Tips for South Wales Visitors"

**Pitch formula:**
```
Subject: [Publication Name] - Guest Post Idea

Hi [Editor],

I'd like to contribute a guest article about [topic]. 
My article would be: "[Title]"

It would guide your readers on [benefit to their audience].

Would you be interested? I can have it ready in [timeframe].

Best regards,
[Your Name]
URL: executivetravel.wales
```

**Time Required:** 15-20 hours (pitching + writing)
**Link Value:** Very High (industry authority)
**Expected Impact:** 5-10 high-quality links

---

### Phase 5: Broken Link Building (Month 4+)
**Goal:** Find broken links on relevant sites and suggest yours

**How it works:**
1. Find competitors' or industry websites
2. Identify broken links on their pages
3. Email site owner: "Hey, found broken link on your page. I have similar content at [your URL]"

**Tools:**
- SEMrush (free trial)
- Ahrefs (free trial)
- Can also do manually by clicking links on relevant sites

**Example email:**
```
Subject: Broken Link on Your [Page Name] Article

Hi [Name],

I was reading your article on [topic] and noticed 
this resource is broken: [broken link].

I have similar content that might work as a replacement:
[Your URL]

Feel free to link if it's relevant!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Time Required:** 10 hours
**Link Value:** High
**Expected Impact:** 5-10 links

## 💰 Paid Link Building Options

If you have budget, these are legitimate:

1. **Sponsorships** £100-500/month
   - Sponsor local events, charities, sports teams
   - Get link in their website + newsletters
   - Also builds brand awareness

2. **Press Release Distribution** £50-150
   - Distribute via PRWeb or similar
   - Gets picked up by news sites
   - Generates links + media coverage

3. **Guest Post Services** £100-300/post
   - Services like Outreach or Authority Hacker
   - Guaranteed placement on relevant sites

**Avoid:**
- Link buying services (Google penalties)
- Link farms and low-quality networks
- Pay-per-link schemes on dodgy sites
- Buying links from unrelated industries

3. Enter competitor domain
4. Check "Backlinks" section
5. Note which sites link to them
6. Pitch those same sites

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't buy links** - Google penalizes this heavily
2. **Don't submit to thousands of directories** - Quality over quantity
3. **Don't use automated tools** - Looks spammy, ignored
4. **Don't post on unrelated sites** - Links from irrelevant sites don't help
5. **Don't ignore natural links** - When people like your content, they link
6. **Don't forget to update links** - Check quarterly if links still active

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start conversations, not pitches** - Personalize every outreach
2. **Offer value first** - "Here's something useful for your audience"
3. **Think long-term** - Relationships matter more than one-off links
4. **Document everything** - Track what works for your business
5. **Be patient** - Links take time to help rankings (2-8 weeks)
6. **Combine strategies** - Directories + partnerships + content is best mix
