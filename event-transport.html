Event Transport FAQs

What's included in event transport?

Our packages include the vehicle, professional driver, fuel, and parking. Optional extras include welcome signs, specific music playlists, and refreshments. Discuss your preferences when booking.

Can you accommodate multiple stops?

Yes! We can arrange event itineraries with multiple stops - hotel to venue to celebration venue, etc. Just outline your plan and we'll create a tailored schedule.

Do you offer group discounts?

We offer 15-20% group discounts for large bookings (40+ passengers). The more guests you have, the better the rate. Call us for a personalised quote.

What if our event runs late?

We're flexible! If your event runs over time, we can extend the booking. Simply let us know in advance or call your driver for last-minute adjustments.

Can we drink alcohol on the bus?

Alcoholic beverages can be enjoyed onboard during celebrations. However, passengers must remain well-behaved. Our drivers reserve the right to refuse service to anyone intoxicated or disruptive.
