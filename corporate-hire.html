Corporate FAQs

Do you offer bulk discounts for regular bookings?

Yes! We provide special rates for companies with regular transport needs. Monthly contracts and bulk bookings receive 15-20% discounts. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

Can drivers accommodate special requests?

Absolutely. From route preferences to passenger assistance, our drivers are trained to handle all requests professionally. We can arrange WiFi, refreshments, specific music, or any other requirements you mention.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks ahead for corporate events, but we can accommodate shorter notice if vehicles are available. Emergency bookings are often possible - just call us.

What sizes of coach can you provide?

We offer minibuses for 8-16 passengers and coaches for up to 49 passengers. Mixed fleet solutions available for very large groups.
