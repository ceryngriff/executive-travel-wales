Minibus Hire FAQs

How much does minibus hire cost?

Pricing depends on distance, duration, and timing. A typical half-day hire for 10-15 people costs £400-600. We offer free quotes - call us with your details and get an instant price.

Can I hire a minibus for a full day?

Absolutely! We offer half-day, full-day, and multi-day minibus hire. Full-day bookings (up to 10 hours) are very popular for corporate days out and group holidays.

Do you do self-drive minibus rental?

No, all our minibuses come with a professional driver included. This is safer, gives you peace of mind, and our drivers know the best routes across Wales.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel for free up to 7 days before your booking. Cancellations within 7 days may incur a fee. Rescheduling is always free.

Can we stop for food or activities?

Yes! We can arrange stops wherever you like - lunch breaks, attraction visits, shopping stops. Tell us your itinerary and we'll make it work.
