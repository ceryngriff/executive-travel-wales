Experience · Reliability · Trust

At Evans Taxis & Executive Travel Wales, reliability comes first. Founder Evan brings more than 20 years of experience in the taxi and private hire industry, building a service recognised for professionalism, safety and trust across South Wales.

Our DBS-checked drivers operate a modern fleet — from local taxis to executive Mercedes vehicles and minibuses — ensuring every journey is comfortable and secure.

Whether you're booking a 24/7 local taxi, an airport transfer, or a tailored chauffeur experience, we guarantee punctuality and peace of mind. You'll also benefit from card payments, advance bookings and live tracking on request.

  • Based in South Wales, covering Merthyr Tydfil, Cardiff, Newport & the Valleys
  • Corporate accounts available with monthly invoicing
  • Fixed-price airport transfers to all major UK airports
  • Child seats & accessibility options available on request
